MIT maps

Hallway C.1–C.9 and Breakthrough C.0: Copyright (c) 2021 Daniel Klingel (CorellanStoma). The Hallway series is a new interpretation of Izzy's Hallway concept. The original MIT notice and full license are included.

GPL maps and conversion sources

Triangle Hell and Empty Ocean are by Lancilloty, distributed under GNU GPL version 3. Triangle Hell is inspired by Hallway 2.0. Their VXL sources and original author metadata are available above, without additional restrictions. Empty Ocean is a blank template for building maps.

The adaptations preserve geometry and surface colors, reverse the vertical axis and fill hidden solid colors from the nearest vertical surface. Their conversion date is 2 October 2026. The adapted GPL maps remain under GPL-3.0. Spawn regions use the authors' bases or team areas, adapted to the converted terrain. Ace of Spades scripts, CTF objectives and water damage are not imported; UBERCUBE uses its own TDM/FFA rules.

Reproduce the conversion with Bun 1.3.11 using the conversion script, map codec and instructions. These conversion sources are supplied under GPL-3.0 with the maps.

Original UBERCUBE resources

The historical models, sounds, textures and font are from Team Ubercube. Their supplied GPL-3.0 license is included. Ragdoll physics uses cannon-es under its MIT license.
