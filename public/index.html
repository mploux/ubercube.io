About the game ↓

A destructible world. A different fight every time.

UBERCUBE is a free multiplayer first-person shooter set in a voxel world. Dig through terrain, build cover and blast new paths with grenades. The battlefield changes as you play.

Pick your loadout and join the action

Enter a nickname, choose Assault, Sniper or Medic in the lobby, and jump into the match. Use rifles, grenades, a shovel and medical supplies to fight, build and help other players. Matches support Team Deathmatch or Free-for-All, with room for up to 100 players on the current server.

Play in your browser

No download, account or payment is required. Play on desktop with a keyboard and mouse, or on mobile with touch controls. If the multiplayer server is unavailable, Play solo lets you explore, shoot and reshape your own world without other players or bots. Each new solo session starts fresh.

How to play

Move with WASD or ZQSD, look with the mouse, jump with Space and sprint with Shift. Left-click to fire, right-click to aim, and use the mouse wheel to switch equipment. Hold and release fire to throw a grenade. With the shovel, left-click to dig and right-click to build.

On mobile, move with the left joystick. Hold and drag the fire button to shoot while looking, or double-tap the right look area and hold the second tap. Tap Aim to toggle your sights. Hold and release to throw a grenade. Jump and change weapons with the on-screen buttons.
